# Minor presentation delivery scope

Recorded 2026-11-19. Presentation: 2026-11-27. Owner now prioritizes a demonstrable complete workflow before the minor presentation, with further research and product improvements in the major project. This is a delivery target, not a guarantee or a claim that remaining research gates passed.

## Minimum demonstration

Citizen submits description, photograph, location and limited explicit contextual facts. Complaint persists through the existing workflow. A versioned experimental text classifier suggests a supported civic family or requests review. A separate transparent prototype policy suggests priority from reported context, with reasons and unknowns. Authorized municipal staff can inspect, accept/correct/defer suggestions and continue the existing resolution/tracking workflow. ML/AI failure must never prevent complaint submission or normal municipal work.

Classification, image observations, severity and operational priority remain separate. Do not force unsupported civic complaints into the small training taxonomy. Do not claim live traffic, verified incident age or municipal urgency ground truth. Submission time is not incident onset.

## Scope cuts and honesty

Do not add a general chatbot, train an all-category image model, fabricate paired text/image incidents, or present a severity predictor as validated without appropriate labels. A pretrained vision API, if selected, is external AI assistance and must be named as such; it is not CivicAI's trained image model. External content cannot change roles/status/priority automatically. No provider is selected or paid service purchased by this plan.

Presentation success means a working observable workflow, real bounded text inference, transparent experimental priority recommendations, human control, reproducible evidence and candid limitations. Robust all-India/multilingual generalization, independent large-scale evaluation, calibrated image severity and multimodal research remain major-project work. If source rights/privacy block real training, report that blocker immediately rather than quietly using unsafe data or passing a toy model off as trained research.
